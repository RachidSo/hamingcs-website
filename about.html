ABOUT

Built from two sides of the same problem.

Hamingcs brings together AI and data leadership with enterprise systems and security architecture — because in most organizations, those two skill sets rarely sit in the same room.

OUR BACKGROUND

Two decades-plus of enterprise experience, from opposite ends of delivery

One half of our practice comes from leading AI and data functions inside a global industrial group — building teams, setting data strategy, and driving AI programs across Europe, Asia, and the Middle East. The other comes from architecting and securing the enterprise systems and SaaS platforms that those programs, and others like them, ultimately have to run on: cloud infrastructure, DevOps, and security engineering across automotive, banking, and technology clients. Hamingcs combines both under one practice.
